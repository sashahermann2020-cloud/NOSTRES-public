# Supporting NOSTRES

NOSTRES is a free, independent guide for people who have moved abroad. It has no ads, no paid placements, no commissions and no user accounts, and it will stay free for its users.

Поддержка NOSTRES: сервис бесплатный для пользователей и останется таким. Работа над ним держится на добровольной поддержке.

## What support pays for

- Research and regular re-checking of routes against official sources: laws, agency pages, forms and fees change, and every route carries the date of its last check.
- Development, accessibility and testing with real users.
- Translations of the interface into eight languages and their review by native speakers.
- Hosting and the domain.
- New cities and countries: after Berlin, routes for Italy and Spain.

## Independence rule

NOSTRES names concrete providers in its routes (banks, health insurers, mobile operators and others), taken only from their own official pages. To keep these routes neutral:

- **A company that appears, or could appear, as a provider option in NOSTRES routes cannot be a sponsor.**
- Support never buys a place, a mention, an order or a recommendation in a route.
- Sponsors do not see user data. NOSTRES does not collect names, addresses or document numbers, and answers stay in the user's browser.
- Organisations and companies that support the project are listed publicly with the period of their support.

Welcome as sponsors: individuals, foundations, non-profit organisations, companies that hire international staff, and technology companies offering services in kind (hosting, translation, testing).

## How to support

Support NOSTRES through Open Collective: <https://opencollective.com/nostres>. Contributions go through Open Source Europe, a Belgian non-profit association (ASBL) that acts as the fiscal host: the money is received and paid out by the host, and every contribution and expense is public on the collective's page.

Organisations and companies that want to discuss support, an invoice or support in kind can write to <alexandra.hermann@nostres.world>.
